Kevin Rozmiarek appeared in the Norwegian documentary series "På tykk is" (On Thick Ice), which explores climate change and research in the Arctic. The documentary featured his work with uncrewed aircraft systems for atmospheric sampling in polar environments.

The series, produced by NRK (Norwegian Broadcasting Corporation), highlights the critical research being conducted in the Arctic to understand climate change and its impacts on ice sheets, permafrost, and polar ecosystems.
