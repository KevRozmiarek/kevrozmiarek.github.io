The Chronicle of Higher Education featured the innovative use of drones at the University of Colorado Boulder, highlighting how researchers like Kevin Rozmiarek are using uncrewed aircraft systems for scientific research.

From atmospheric sampling in Greenland to supporting emergency management, drones have become essential tools for research and operations at CU Boulder. The article explores how universities are integrating drone technology into their research programs and the regulatory challenges and opportunities this presents.
