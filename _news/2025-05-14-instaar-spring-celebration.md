INSTAAR held its annual spring celebration luncheon to honor outstanding students and faculty members for their contributions to Arctic and alpine research. The event recognized achievements in research, teaching, and service to the institute and the broader scientific community.

The celebration highlighted the important work being done at INSTAAR to understand climate change, permafrost dynamics, and Arctic ecosystems, bringing together researchers, students, and staff to celebrate another successful year of groundbreaking science.
